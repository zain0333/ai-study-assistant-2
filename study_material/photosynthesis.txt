Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants make their own food using sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide.

Chlorophyll is the green pigment in plant leaves that absorbs sunlight.

Plants take in carbon dioxide from the air through tiny openings in their leaves called stomata. Their roots absorb water from the soil.

Using sunlight energy, plants convert water and carbon dioxide into glucose and oxygen.

The basic photosynthesis equation is:

Carbon dioxide + Water + Light Energy → Glucose + Oxygen

Glucose provides energy for plants and helps them grow. Oxygen is released into the atmosphere and is used by humans and animals for respiration.

Photosynthesis is important because it provides food for plants, releases oxygen, and helps maintain the balance of carbon dioxide and oxygen in the atmosphere.
